# Dreamlytics FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

---

## General

**Q: What is Dreamlytics?**
A: Dreamlytics is an AI-powered dream analysis and journaling platform for web and mobile. It helps users log, analyze, and reflect on their dreams using advanced language models and provides analytics, motif/emotion extraction, and a public dream feed.

**Q: Who can use Dreamlytics?**
A: Anyone interested in dream journaling, analysis, or exploring dream patterns. The app is designed for both personal and community use.

---

## Getting Started

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click on the Register button, fill in your email, username, and password, then follow the instructions to verify your account.

**Q: What are the system requirements?**
A: You need a modern browser for the web app. For mobile, Android 8.0+ is recommended. Node.js and npm are required for local development.

---

## Features & Usage

**Q: How do I log a dream?**
A: After logging in, go to the Dreams section and click "New Dream". Fill in the title, content, date, tags, and choose if it should be public or private.

**Q: How does AI dream analysis work?**
A: When you submit a dream, the app uses large language models (LLMs) to analyze the content, extract motifs and emotions, and provide a summary. You can view the analysis in your dream journal.

**Q: Can I share my dreams with others?**
A: Yes, you can mark dreams as public. These will appear in the community feed, where other users can view and like them.

**Q: What analytics are available?**
A: You can view statistics such as emotion timelines, motif frequency, and dream frequency in the Analytics section.

---

## Privacy & Security

**Q: Are my dreams private?**
A: By default, dreams are private and only visible to you. You can choose to share dreams publicly if you wish.

**Q: How is my data protected?**
A: Dreamlytics uses secure authentication and stores data in a protected database. Sensitive information is never shared without your consent.

**Q: Is my data used for AI training?**
A: No, your personal dream data is not used to train external AI models. Analysis is performed in real-time using third-party APIs.

## Technical & Troubleshooting

**Q: I can't log in or register. What should I do?**
A: Check your internet connection, ensure your credentials are correct, and try resetting your password. If issues persist, contact support or open a GitHub issue.

**Q: The AI analysis is not working.**
A: Make sure your account is active and you have a valid API key configured (for developers). If the problem continues, check the server logs or contact support.

**Q: How do I build the mobile app?**
A: See the Developer Guide for instructions on building and deploying the Android app using Capacitor and Android Studio.
